06.

Discounts.

Assign a percentage-off code to an influencer. They can share the code itself, or share the QR code — followers who scan land on a pre-filled booking page. Add their email if you want the system to auto-send them a branded email with the QR attached; leave it blank if you'll just text the QR image yourself (open the QR / link modal after saving to grab the PNG). Each redemption sends you an email so you know which influencer is driving bookings.

Codes can have a term limit (good until a date) and a usage cap (good for the first N people).
